Jordan EJ11 (2001) — initial personal test build 0.1.0

Source: Owen supplied Downloads/2001-jordan-ej11/source/jordan_ej11.glb (48 material meshes). Original author/licence absent from supplied GLB asset metadata. No original source files changed. See CREDITS.txt.

Geometry: source is in metre-scale coordinates; preserve uniform scale 1.0. Source measured axle spacing 3.129332691 m, total width 1.80486 m, length 4.63888 m. Four tyres recovered as connected components of the merged TYRE material. Mirror X into FH2 coordinates and shift Y -0.1801696 m / Z +0.43889135 m; near-zero lateral centre corrected. Rim-face tracks measured from the exposed front/rear rim geometry (1.733 / 1.777 m before database factor 1.012), not nonexistent wheel arches. Runtime tyre centres within 30 mm of source and spindle heights within 5 mm. Nominal rim radius 0.1651 m (13 inches), source lip radius 0.166917 m. Provisional nominal tyres 300/55R13 front and 355/46R13 rear chosen to preserve source fit, not asserted original EJ11 tyre specifications. Game OW_F1 tyre model; original tyre lettering and blurred rim decal discs omitted. Source rim geometry used for all four runtime rims; rear width morph remains approximate.

Materials: source livery retained at 1024-pixel game texture size; cabin/mechanics at 512. Transparent sponsor planes clipped against their alpha mask and edge colours extended, avoiding solid opaque rectangles. Decals moved 2 mm proud to prevent z-fighting after vertex packing. Preserve UV seams. Single-sided body geometry using source normals. Source cockpit windscreen uses game glass. Dedicated steering materials moved from body to steering spindle part for steering animation; instrument artwork remains static. Driver uses the existing local STR1 low race-car pose, shifted into this seat; LMP_OpenCockpit template. Cockpit eye, driver pose and helmet/hand clearance require game review.

Powertrain: Honda official sources confirm RA001E 3-litre V10, 108 kg engine, 17,300 rpm and Jordan's own seven-speed gearbox. https://global.honda/en/POWEREDbyHONDA/2001_ra001e/ and https://global.honda/en/newsroom/worldnews/2001/c010116.html . 2,994 cc, about 788 bhp / 340 Nm, torque shape, individual ratios and final drive are provisional game settings. Full v0..v245 schema filled, positive zero-throttle value, 175 entries and cut marker v174=-2.63; no old 12,000-rpm compression. BMW_P83_03 V10 audio from the already installed Williams FW24 is a placeholder, not a Honda engine recording. Manifest depends on zs-williams-fw24. 600 kg target mass, RWD, Formula shift mode, dedicated stock race components and tuning enabled. Road-car non-stock upgrades removed from this car's own rows. Handling, mass distribution, suspension, aero and PI require in-game tuning; use established personal STR1 / installed FW24 game values as references, not a certified physics recreation.

Collision: own hull generated from body, floor, carbon and wings (half-width .72 m, floor -.118 m), excluding wheel/cockpit detail meshes. Rear light: single central lamp with tail/brake functions; no road headlights, indicators or reverse lamps. This does not implement an authentic flashing F1 rain-light control.

Presentation: Jordan manufacturer logo and Autoshow thumbnail, Extreme Track Toys category (same bucket as installed Williams), unique car/engine/drivetrain 4201, strings 65100-65102, media JOR_JordanEJ11_01_. Existing installed car IDs/strings checked for conflicts. Insert-only database diff; no stock car edits.

Checks: SQLite integrity; V10 identity; full curve and cutoff; 7 gears/Formula shift; mass/tyre model; post-database measured fit; all model parts decode; texture path lengths, CRCs and archive targets; archive CRC; steering-local bounds; collision dimensions; two central light records; manifest and sound dependency. Fourteen wheel-fit regression checks passed, including exposed rims and rejection of a wrong track. Actual game rendering, idle/driving, steering, lights and cockpit still need Owen's first game check.

Build: prepare_source.py, then package_car.py cars/jordanej11/spec.py output. race_setup.py and rain_light.py supply car-specific database and lighting adjustments. Current working source is under Passat B3 Build/cars/jordanej11.
