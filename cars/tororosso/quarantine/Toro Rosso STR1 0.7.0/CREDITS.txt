Scuderia Toro Rosso STR1 (2006) for Forza Horizon 2 - Owen's personal mod. NOT FOR REDISTRIBUTION.

Car model, textures/livery and physics reference data:
  CTDP - Cars & Tracks Development Project, "CTDP F1 2006 for rFactor" (c) 2008-2010 CTDP.
  Used for this personal, non-distributed conversion with CTDP's permission (obtained by Owen).
  The model files came via an Assetto Corsa conversion ("acfl_2006_str") uploaded to Sketchfab as
  "2006 Scuderia Toro Rosso STR1" by Dave Love; the original work is CTDP's.
Physics figures (mass, weight distribution, wheelbase, engine torque curve, gearing) from CTDP's STR1 setup files.
Base car in FH2: 2012 Dodge Charger SRT8 (wheels, brakes, steering column). Engine sound: Porsche Carrera GT V10.
Team names, sponsor logos and trademarks belong to their owners; shown only in this private build.

0.7.0 update: original CTDP STR1 model, livery, wheel meshes and TJ2005 engine data retained for Owen's personal use. Digital gauge layout/shift-light resources adapted from the installed ZS Williams FW24 package. Native carbon/digital-gauge material instances referenced from that package. BMW P83 sound is an approximation supplied by the required zs-williams-fw24 mod; it is not an authentic Cosworth TJ2005 recording. No Williams body, livery or steering-wheel mesh is substituted.
