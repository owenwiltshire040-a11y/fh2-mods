Peugeot 208 PureTech 130 (2021) for Forza Horizon 2 - Owen's personal mod.

Car model: "2021 Peugeot 208" by tonielpro520, https://sketchfab.com/ (Sketchfab), licensed CC BY 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Converted from the SketchUp OBJ; wheels, tyres and brakes are the
  game's own (the model's were not used), the body paint uses the game's paint material.
Physics figures from Peugeot's published 208 II 1.2 PureTech 130 GT EAT8 data; suspension and anti-roll bars scaled
  from the game's Ford Fiesta Mk7 (not published by Peugeot).
Base car in FH2: 2012 Dodge Charger SRT8 (skeleton, driver, wheels, brakes). Engine sound: Fiat 500 Abarth 1.4 Turbo.
